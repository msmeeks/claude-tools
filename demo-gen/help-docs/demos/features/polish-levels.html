About this demo

--polish controls the quality of every element: screenshots, captions, takeaways, video audio, and transitions. We step through the three levels side by side so you can choose the right one for your workflow.

4

Choose the right level for each workflow

$ # CI pipeline: fast draft, HTML only
demo-gen generate --polish draft --format html --output-dir ./ci-preview/

$ # Sprint review: standard, both formats
demo-gen generate --polish standard --format both --output-dir ./sprint-demo/

$ # Release: production, real screenshots, best voice
demo-gen generate --polish production --voice am_adam \
  --screenshot s1.png --screenshot s2.png \
  --format both --output-dir ./releases/v2.0/
Use draft when you need a quick preview in under 10 seconds. Use standard for team review and sprint demos. Reserve production for anything customer-facing or recorded for permanent use.

Key Takeaways

  • draft is the fastest — placeholder mocks, Piper TTS, no SFX, no takeaways section
  • standard adds real screenshots, Kokoro TTS, styled captions, and a plain takeaways list
  • production adds highlight rings, callout labels, gradient takeaways, IntersectionObserver nav, and the best Kokoro voice
  • All three levels produce fully valid HTML and MP4 — the difference is quality, not format
  • You can re-run with a higher polish level at any time — just reuse the same screenshots

## Methodology

This analysis ranks work items as candidates for nevering based on the following scoring system:

### Scoring Criteria

1. **Epic Linkage** (+100 points)
   - Work item is linked to a completed or nevered epic
   - **Rationale**: If the parent epic is done/dropped, child items are likely obsolete

2. **Customer Impact** (+80 points for 0 cases, +60 for <5 cases + old)
   - Zero reported customers or cases
   - Low customer impact (<5 cases) combined with age >180 days
   - **Rationale**: No customer demand indicates low value

3. **Age** (+20 points for >365 days, +10 for >180 days)
   - Work items sitting in backlog for extended periods
   - **Rationale**: Older items are less likely to be prioritized

4. **Inactivity** (+15 points for >180 days, +10 for >90 days)
   - No recent updates or activity
   - **Rationale**: Inactive items suggest low team interest

### Priority Levels

- **Critical (Score ≥ 100)**: Linked to completed/nevered epic - strong candidates for immediate nevering
- **High (Score ≥ 80)**: Zero customer impact - safe to never without customer disruption
- **Medium (Score ≥ 60)**: Low impact + old/inactive - review and consider nevering
- **Lower (Score < 60)**: May still be valuable - review case-by-case

## Limitations & Caveats

1. **Field Availability**: Customer/case count may not be available in GUS API; analysis falls back to age/activity scoring
2. **Permissions**: Only includes work items visible to your GUS account
3. **Epic Status**: Limited to epics available in GUS API at time of analysis
4. **Last Activity**: Approximated from last modified date; may not reflect latest comments
5. **Scoring Thresholds**: Default values (5 cases, 180 days, 90 days) may need adjustment for your team's context
6. **Team Filtering**: Filtered by product tags if direct team filtering is unavailable

**Recommendation**: Review high-scoring items manually before nevering to ensure accuracy.
